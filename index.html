Questions before you order

Where do you deliver?

We deliver across India. Delivery cost and time depend on your city and the size of the product. Share your pin code on WhatsApp and we will tell you.

How do I pay?

We accept UPI and bank transfer. We share the details on WhatsApp once your order is confirmed.

Can I return or exchange a product?

If your product arrives damaged, send us a photo within 48 hours and we will fix it or replace it.

Can you make something custom?

Yes, in many cases. Send us what you have in mind and we will tell you if we can make it, how long it takes and what it costs.

How long does an order take?

Items in stock ship in 2 to 3 days. Made-to-order items take longer and we confirm the time before you pay.

Ready to order?

WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach us. We usually reply within a few hours.
